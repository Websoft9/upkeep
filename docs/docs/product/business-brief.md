# Business Brief

## Working title

Robot Intelligence for Predictive Maintenance

## One-line summary

A software platform that turns industrial robot telemetry into health insights,
early warnings, and maintenance recommendations.

## Problem

Industrial robots generate large volumes of operational data, but most factories
use that data only for alarms, fault codes, and post-incident troubleshooting. As
a result, hidden degradation is missed, maintenance remains reactive, and
avoidable downtime impacts throughput and cost.

## Opportunity

Fixed industrial robots operate in repeatable work cells with stable process
patterns. This makes them well suited for data-driven monitoring, anomaly
detection, and predictive maintenance. The market opportunity is to convert
existing telemetry into actionable intelligence without requiring immediate
changes to the data collection layer.

## Product vision

Make every industrial robot observable, understandable, and serviceable through
software.

## Target customer

- Manufacturers running fixed industrial robot cells
- Operations, maintenance, and reliability teams
- System integrators or equipment service providers supporting robot fleets

## Core value proposition

- Reduce unplanned downtime
- Detect degradation before failure
- Improve maintenance timing and quality
- Increase asset utilization and OEE
- Unlock value from existing machine data

## MVP scope

- Ingesting robot time-series data pushed from external systems
- Modeling robot assets, work cells, and process states
- Detecting anomalies and degradation trends
- Generating explainable maintenance recommendations
- Capturing feedback to improve future accuracy

## What we are not building first

- A full edge data acquisition platform
- Direct robot control or closed-loop automation
- A broad MES or ERP replacement
- A generic AI platform for all industrial assets

## Differentiation

- Built specifically for fixed industrial robot scenarios
- Focused on maintenance intelligence, not just dashboards
- Combines rules, domain skills, and AI models
- Designed to work with existing telemetry providers
- Keeps recommendations explainable and operationally usable

## Go-to-market hypothesis

- Enter the market by shipping the software together with robot equipment
  solutions
- Target smaller robot OEMs or integrators that lack strong software capability
- Position the product first as an intelligence layer that improves equipment
  value and after-sales service
- Use early deployments to build data models, diagnostic skills, and proof of ROI
- Evolve from OEM-attached software into a vendor-neutral platform for customers
  and integrators

## Business outcome

The product helps industrial customers move from reactive robot maintenance to
data-driven maintenance decisions, with lower operational risk and faster time to
value.
