# Product Business Brief

## Working Title

Robot Intelligence for Predictive Maintenance

## One-Line Summary

A software platform that turns industrial robot telemetry into health insights, early warnings, and maintenance recommendations.

## Problem

Industrial robots generate large volumes of operational data, but most factories use that data only for alarms, fault codes, and post-incident troubleshooting. As a result, hidden degradation is missed, maintenance remains reactive, and avoidable downtime impacts throughput and cost.

## Opportunity

Fixed industrial robots operate in repeatable work cells with stable process patterns. This makes them well suited for data-driven monitoring, anomaly detection, and predictive maintenance. The market opportunity is to convert existing telemetry into actionable intelligence without requiring immediate changes to the data collection layer.

## Product Vision

Make every industrial robot observable, understandable, and serviceable through software.

## Target Customer

- Manufacturers running fixed industrial robot cells
- Operations, maintenance, and reliability teams
- System integrators or equipment service providers supporting robot fleets

## Core Value Proposition

- Reduce unplanned downtime
- Detect degradation before failure
- Improve maintenance timing and quality
- Increase asset utilization and OEE
- Unlock value from existing machine data

## MVP Scope

The first version focuses on:

- ingesting robot time-series data pushed from external systems
- modeling robot assets, work cells, and process states
- detecting anomalies and degradation trends
- generating explainable maintenance recommendations
- capturing feedback to improve future accuracy

## What We Are Not Building First

- a full edge data acquisition platform
- direct robot control or closed-loop automation
- a broad MES or ERP replacement
- a generic AI platform for all industrial assets

## Differentiation

- Built specifically for fixed industrial robot scenarios
- Focused on maintenance intelligence, not just dashboards
- Combines rules, domain skills, and AI models
- Designed to work with existing telemetry providers
- Keeps recommendations explainable and operationally usable

## Go-To-Market Hypothesis

- Enter the market by shipping the software together with robot equipment solutions
- Target smaller robot OEMs or integrators that lack strong software capability
- Position the product first as an intelligence layer that improves equipment value and after-sales service
- Use early deployments to build data models, diagnostic skills, and proof of ROI
- Evolve from OEM-attached software into a vendor-neutral platform for customers and integrators

## Business Outcome

The product helps industrial customers move from reactive robot maintenance to data-driven maintenance decisions, with lower operational risk and faster time to value.
